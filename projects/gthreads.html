GThreads

A private local-first Gmail case workspace concept for turning messy inbox trails into named support cases, notes, and structured context without taking control away from the operator.

Private prototype Gmail case workspace Local-first direction Support context

The problem

Support work often hides inside email threads. One customer issue may include multiple replies, forwarded context, vendor messages, technician updates, and notes that are hard to connect later.

Inbox friction Threads are searchable, but not naturally structured as cases.
Context loss Important details can disappear inside long trails or forwarded messages.
Operator need Keep Gmail familiar while adding case names, notes, and stronger context.

System shape

The direction is a local-first case workspace that links Gmail threads into named cases, adds notes, preserves operator control, and turns email context into something easier to revisit during support work.

What this shows

Support workflow design Seeing the operational shape behind messy email trails.
Google ecosystem thinking Using Gmail context without forcing the team into an unfamiliar workflow.
Product restraint Building structure around the operator instead of replacing the operator.
Sanitized case preview No email content or customer data

Gmail threads turned into named support cases, notes, and clean context.
