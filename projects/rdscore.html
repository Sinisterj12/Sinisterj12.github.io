Operational intelligence · support systems

RDSCore

A support operations workspace shaped by live helpdesk pressure: search-first context, ticket momentum, ownership, store relationships and selective AI assistance only after the underlying information is trustworthy enough to support it.

Support operationsSearch-first contextCRM workspaceHuman-centered AISanitized demo data

Problem

Support teams lose time before troubleshooting even starts.

Store data, contacts, vendor notes, assets, tickets, install history and repeat issues often live in disconnected systems. During a live escalation, technicians spend valuable time rebuilding context instead of solving the problem.

System direction

Build a reliable context layer before adding AI.

RDSCore is designed as a command surface for support work: fast search, visible workload, ownership, ticket context and relationship-aware store information. AI is a later layer, used selectively where it can summarize or reason over context that already has a trustworthy source.

Why AI is not the first layer

Bad retrieval creates confident nonsense faster.

If store context is incomplete or mixed across customers, a language model can make the situation worse by turning uncertainty into a plausible answer. RDSCore therefore treats search quality, source boundaries and structured relationships as prerequisites for AI interpretation.

Human / AI boundary

The technician remains the operator.

AI can help with

  • Summarizing known support history
  • Surfacing related repeat issues
  • Organizing technical context
  • Suggesting likely next checks from available evidence

Human owns

  • Diagnosis and escalation decisions
  • Customer-impacting changes
  • Interpretation when evidence conflicts
  • Final resolution and documentation quality

Privacy & proof

Show the system without exposing customer data.

Public screenshots use approved demo information. The portfolio demonstrates interface direction and operational reasoning while keeping private store history, customer data and internal support details out of the public site.

What this demonstrates

AI deployment starts with systems thinking.

RDSCore shows how I translate domain knowledge into an AI-ready workflow: identify the operational bottleneck, model trustworthy context, design the operator experience, define where AI adds leverage, and preserve human responsibility where mistakes carry real consequences.
