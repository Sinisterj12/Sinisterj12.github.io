RDSCore CRM Workspace

A private CRM wrapper and support operations workspace concept built around the real friction of fast store lookup, ticket context, contacts, vendor references, assets, repeat issues, and escalation history.

RDSCore in motion

These are live UI screenshots from the CRM workspace using non-sensitive demo/test information. They show the race-style support dashboard first, then the priority action deck where ticket work moves into review.

The problem

Support operators lose time when store context, contacts, tickets, vendor references, install notes, assets, and repeat-issue history live in scattered places. The idea behind RDSCore is to turn that daily friction into one cleaner command surface.

Support pain Slow lookup during live store pressure.
Operational risk Important context can be missed when teams jump between tools.
AI opportunity Use AI selectively after the search and context layer is trustworthy.

System shape

The working direction is a FastAPI-backed operations workspace with structured search routing, graph-style relationships, runtime contracts, and careful AI escalation. The goal is not a decorative dashboard. The goal is a support command surface that helps a technician understand a store faster.

Public page content stays sanitized because the real workflow touches internal operations, customer support history, and private store context.

What this shows

Workflow design Turning support lookup friction into a specific operating surface.
Data modeling Thinking in relationships between stores, contacts, assets, vendors, and tickets.
AI judgment Keeping AI behind context and proof instead of making it the whole product.
Sanitized CRM preview No private customer or store data

Fast lookup, relationship context, and support history in one operator surface.
