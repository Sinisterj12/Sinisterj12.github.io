About

Principles

How I build

No fake confidence

I do not guess when the system can prove it. Logs, tests, screenshots, receipts, runtime checks, and real operator feedback beat pretty assumptions.

AI is leverage, not autopilot

I let agents move fast, but I still define the target, review the result, and build guardrails so speed does not turn into chaos.

Systems should reduce friction

Good tools remove repeat pain, improve clarity, shorten decisions, and hold up under pressure when phones are ringing and stores need answers.
